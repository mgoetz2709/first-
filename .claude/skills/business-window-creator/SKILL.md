# Business Window Creator

Structure company information into a Business Window format for B2B sales preparation.

## Business Window Structure

The Business Window consists of four categories:

1. **Strategic Goals (What?)** - Company objectives, growth targets, expansion plans, network development, market penetration goals
2. **Products, Markets, Services (With What?)** - Main products, target markets, services offered, solutions provided
3. **Philosophies (How?)** - Values, vision, mission, ethical principles, sustainability focus, company culture
4. **Organization (With Whom?)** - Leadership team, founder/CEO, geographic focus, headquarters location, company size, organizational structure

## Workflow

1. **Receive input** - Accept company information from the user (typically from Peter's Pain Analysis and Ralf's Company Profile)

2. **Analyze and categorize** - Review all provided information and assign each piece to the appropriate Business Window category

3. **Verify completeness** - Check that all four categories are addressed; mark categories as "n/a" if no information is available

4. **Structure output** - Present results in the standardized Business Window format (see Output Format below)

## Output Format

Present the Business Window as a structured grid with four fields:

```
Company Name: [Company Name] ([website URL if available])

Strategic Goals (What?):
- [Goal 1]
- [Goal 2]
- [Goal 3]
[or "n/a" if no information available]

Products, Markets, Services (With What?):
Main Products: [List products]
Target Markets: [Describe target markets]
Services: [List services]
[or "n/a" if no information available]

Philosophies (How?):
Values: [List values]
Vision: [State vision]
[Additional philosophical elements as relevant]
[or "n/a" if no information available]

Organization (With Whom?):
Founder/CEO: [Name(s) or "n/a"]
Geographic Focus: [Location/region]
Size: [Team size, organizational structure]
[or "n/a" if no information available]
```

## Quality Standards

- **Accuracy** - Only include information explicitly provided or clearly derivable from the input
- **Clarity** - Use concise, professional language; avoid unnecessary commentary
- **Completeness** - Address all four categories; use "n/a" when information is missing
- **Logical consistency** - Verify that categorized information makes sense and doesn't contradict
- **Professional presentation** - Deliver clean, structured output ready for immediate use in sales preparation

## Important Notes

- Do not add introductory text or explanations before the Business Window
- Do not add commentary after the Business Window unless specifically requested
- Focus solely on structuring the provided information into the correct categories
- If information could fit multiple categories, choose the most logical placement
- Maintain a data-driven, objective tone throughout
